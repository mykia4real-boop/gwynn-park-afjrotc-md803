MD-803 · 2025–2026 CADET HANDBOOK

Cadet Handbook

A detailed, cadet-friendly reference for the standards, expectations, ranks, leadership principles, uniform rules, recognition, and everyday information used in Gwynn Park High School AFJROTC.

Start Here

The purpose and foundation of MD-803.

AFJROTC Mission

Develop citizens of character dedicated to serving their nation and community.

The program develops citizenship, service, personal responsibility, accomplishment, leadership, and self-discipline.

Cadet Responsibility

The handbook is a day-to-day reference for the cadet corps. Cadets are responsible for knowing its information, understanding its standards, and putting those standards into practice.

Cadet Code & Creed

Cadets are expected to live with honesty and accountability, bring credit to their family, school, corps, community, and themselves, and uphold the Air Force Core Values.

Leadership, Courtesy & Conduct

Standards apply in AFJROTC and beyond the classroom.

Lead by Example

  • Show proper conduct and appearance in and out of uniform.
  • Respect instructors, teachers, staff, cadet leaders, peers, parents, and other adults.
  • Develop followership, leadership, responsibility, judgment, teamwork, and self-discipline.
  • Your conduct in other classes still reflects on you as a cadet.

Classroom Standards

  • Be prompt and ready before instruction begins.
  • Follow flight procedures and instructor directions.
  • Stay attentive; classroom distractions and sleeping are not acceptable.
  • Use military courtesies appropriately, including respectful Sir/Ma’am responses.

Hazing & Misuse of Authority

Hazing is prohibited. Cadets may not use humiliation, abusive language, coercion, or physical activity as punishment or initiation. Cadet authority must be used responsibly and respectfully.

Uniform Wear & Appearance

The uniform represents the United States Air Force and must be worn with pride.

Uniform Day

The SASI determines the prescribed uniform day. When assigned, the uniform is worn for the entire school day, not only during the AFJROTC class period, unless an instructor grants an exception.

Grooming & Appearance

Cadets follow applicable Air Force grooming and appearance standards while in uniform. Keep the uniform clean, properly fitted, correctly arranged, and as sharp as possible.

Need Placement Help?

Use the separate Uniform Guide for visual placement of insignia and uniform items. When unsure, ask cadet group staff or an AFJROTC instructor.

Five Cadet Essentials

Specific answers to the things cadets are most likely to ask about.

Aerospace & Leadership

WHAT YOU DEVELOP

Citizenship and life skills, leadership and followership, personal responsibility, self-discipline, communication, teamwork, military customs and courtesies, and an understanding of air and space fundamentals.

WHY IT MATTERS

The program is designed to prepare capable civilian and military leaders and expose cadets to aerospace and military career possibilities.

Community Service

PROGRAM EXPECTATION

Community service is part of AFJROTC’s mission and objectives. Cadets should participate professionally, represent MD-803 well, and follow event leadership instructions.

KEEP TRACK

Use the site’s Service Hours area for your recorded service information and follow unit directions for documenting participation.

Fitness Standards

FITNESS CHECK

Cadets are tested twice per school year.

ACTIVITIES
  • Push-ups
  • Sit-ups
  • One-mile run

Fitness activities are part of supervised wellness—not punishment or hazing.

Core Values

INTEGRITY FIRST

Do what is right and be honest and accountable.

SERVICE BEFORE SELF

Put duty and the needs of the team ahead of convenience.

EXCELLENCE IN ALL WE DO

Work toward high standards in academics, leadership, uniform wear, service, and conduct.

Mission & Objectives

MISSION

Develop citizens of character dedicated to serving their nation and community.

OBJECTIVES

Educate and train students in citizenship and life skills, promote community service, instill responsibility, and develop character and self-discipline through air and space education and the Core Values.

Awards & Recognition

Recognition is tied to achievement, leadership, service, academics, performance, and participation.

Ribbons & Medals

AFJROTC awards recognize cadet achievement and build morale and esprit de corps. Specific awards have their own eligibility and wear criteria.

Badges

Only authorized badges and insignia may be worn. Some badges are earned through specific programs or performance requirements.

Shoulder Cords

Authorized shoulder cords identify approved positions or activities. Wear and placement must follow unit and AFJROTC guidance.
