The purpose and foundation of MD-803.

AFJROTC Mission

Develop citizens of character dedicated to serving their nation and community.

Cadet Responsibility

The handbook is a day-to-day reference for the cadet corps.

Cadet Code & Creed

Cadets are expected to live with honesty, accountability, and the Air Force Core Values.

Leadership, Courtesy & Conduct

Standards apply in AFJROTC and beyond the classroom.

Lead by Example

Show proper conduct and appearance, respect others, and develop leadership and self-discipline.

Classroom Standards

Be prompt, prepared, attentive, respectful, and follow flight procedures.

Hazing & Misuse of Authority

Hazing is prohibited. Cadet authority must be used responsibly and respectfully.

Uniform Wear & Appearance

The uniform represents the United States Air Force and must be worn with pride.

Uniform Day

The prescribed uniform is worn for the entire school day unless an instructor grants an exception.

Grooming & Appearance

Follow applicable grooming and appearance standards and keep the uniform neat and correct.

Need Placement Help?

Use the Uniform Guide for visual placement guidance.

Five Cadet Essentials

Specific answers to common cadet questions.

Aerospace & Leadership

Citizenship, leadership, followership, communication, teamwork, military customs and courtesies, and air and space fundamentals.

Community Service

Community service is part of AFJROTC’s mission. Cadets should represent MD-803 professionally and document participation as directed.

Fitness Standards

Cadets are tested twice per school year in push-ups, sit-ups, and the one-mile run.

Core Values

Integrity First. Service Before Self. Excellence In All We Do.

Mission & Objectives

Develop citizens of character while building citizenship, service, responsibility, leadership, and self-discipline.

Awards & Recognition

Recognition is tied to achievement, leadership, service, academics, performance, and participation.

Ribbons & Medals

Authorized awards recognize cadet achievement and service.

Badges

Only authorized badges and insignia may be worn.

Shoulder Cords

Authorized shoulder cords identify approved positions or activities.
