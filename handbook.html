Cadet Handbook

Your guide to MD-803 standards, expectations, opportunities, and cadet life.

Welcome to MD-803

The handbook is the day-to-day reference for standards and expectations across the cadet corps.

Cadet Responsibility

Cadets are expected to know the handbook, understand its standards, and put those standards into practice in daily cadet life.

Cadet Code

The Cadet Code centers on honesty, accountability, respect, service, and refusing to tolerate lying, cheating, or stealing.

Cadet Creed

The creed connects cadets to service, patriotism, accountability, leadership, Wingmanship, and the Air Force Core Values.

Program Overview

What AFJROTC is designed to develop in every cadet.

Mission

Develop citizens of character dedicated to serving their nation and community.

Program Goals

  • Citizenship and service to the United States
  • Personal responsibility and accomplishment
  • Academic success and leadership development
  • Military drill, customs, and courtesies

AFJROTC Benefits

AFJROTC carries no military obligation. The handbook also explains possible advanced-entry benefits for eligible cadets who complete multiple years of JROTC and later choose military service or college ROTC.

Leadership, Conduct & Standards

Cadets are expected to lead by example in and outside AFJROTC.

Conduct

  • Show proper behavior and appearance in and out of uniform.
  • Demonstrate respect toward instructors, staff, cadet leaders, peers, and adults.
  • Use military courtesies and professional responses appropriately.
  • Set the example across all classes, not only AFJROTC.

Leadership

  • Develop followership before and while developing leadership.
  • Build self-discipline and group discipline.
  • Practice initiative, responsibility, judgment, communication, and teamwork.
  • Respect authority and use cadet authority responsibly.

Hazing

Hazing is prohibited. Cadets may not use physical activity, humiliation, abusive language, or coercion as punishment or initiation. Physical training belongs in supervised wellness activities, not discipline.

Uniform Standards

Wear the Air Force uniform properly, with pride, and for the full prescribed school day.

Uniform Wear

The SASI determines the uniform wear day and prescribed uniform. Cadets are expected to wear the assigned uniform for the entire school day unless an instructor grants an exception.

Appearance

Cadets must follow applicable grooming and appearance standards while in uniform and maintain the sharpest appearance possible.

Placement Help

Use the separate Uniform Guide for visual placement. If unsure about grooming or insignia placement, consult cadet group staff or an AFJROTC instructor.

AEROSPACE & LEADERSHIP

WHAT THE HANDBOOK SAYS YOU WILL DEVELOP
  • Understand the aerospace environment and how aerospace technology affects society and the nation.
  • Explore civilian and military aerospace career opportunities.
  • Learn Air Force and military traditions, drill, customs, and courtesies.
  • Master effective followership and leadership qualities.
  • Build self-discipline, group discipline, personal honor, and self-reliance.
  • Strengthen interest in completing high school and pursuing higher education.
WHY IT MATTERS

The program is designed to help cadets become poised, capable, effective leaders in civilian or military settings.

COMMUNITY SERVICE

PROGRAM EXPECTATION
  • Community service is one of the stated objectives of AFJROTC.
  • Service should build citizenship, responsibility, character, and a habit of contributing to the community.
  • Use the Service Hours area of the site to keep your participation organized and documented.
  • Follow unit instructions for what activities count and what proof or approval is required.
HOW TO LOG HOURS ON THIS SITE
  1. Open Service Hours.
  2. Add the service date, organization, hours, and description.
  3. Submit the entry so staff can review the record.
  4. Check your dashboard for your updated total.

FITNESS STANDARDS

PT TEST COMPONENTS
  • Push-ups
  • Sit-ups
  • One-mile run
  • Cadets are tested twice per school year.
WHAT TO REMEMBER
  • Your dashboard stores your most recent results so you can see your progress.
  • Physical activity may not be assigned by cadet leaders as punishment.
  • Regular PT and wellness activities must be conducted appropriately and under instructor supervision.
  • Ask an instructor if you are unsure about test procedures, standards, or approved accommodations.

CORE VALUES

INTEGRITY FIRST

Do what is right and be honest. The Cadet Code specifically rejects lying, cheating, and stealing.

SERVICE BEFORE SELF

Put responsibility, the team, and service ahead of personal convenience when leadership requires it.

EXCELLENCE IN ALL WE DO

Apply high standards to academics, conduct, uniform wear, leadership, drill, service, and everyday responsibilities.

ACCOUNTABILITY

The Cadet Creed emphasizes being accountable for your own actions and holding others accountable as well.
